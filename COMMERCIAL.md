# Commercial licensing

**HA Companion for Windows** is free and open source under the **GNU AGPL-3.0-only**.

That license is a great fit for individuals, hobbyists, and other open-source projects. It comes with one firm condition: **if you distribute the software or offer it (or a modified version) as a network service, you must make your complete corresponding source code available under the AGPL, too.**

For many companies that copyleft obligation is a dealbreaker. If you want to:

- ship this in a **closed-source** or proprietary product,
- **bundle** it into a commercial offering,
- run a modified version as a **hosted/SaaS** service without publishing your source,
- or otherwise use it under terms **other than the AGPL**,

then you need a **separate commercial license**. As the sole copyright holder, the maintainer can grant one.
